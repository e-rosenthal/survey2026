Methodology: The normalized view uses within-respondent mean-centering for each rating battery. For every respondent, the dashboard subtracts that person’s average rating across the values or priority items from each item rating, then averages those differences across respondents. A positive score means an item was rated above that respondent’s own baseline; a negative score means below baseline. Raw 1–5 averages remain available by turning this off.
